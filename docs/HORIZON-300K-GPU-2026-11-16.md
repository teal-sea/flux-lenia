# 300,000-step horizon at 50 seeds, with and without food sensing, on GPU (design, 2026-11-16)

Written before any run. Staged for Modal A100 containers (gpu/modal_campaign.py, timeout raised to 6 h
for this experiment); launch needs the owner's yes and a restored Modal workspace.

## Why

Campaign part A (docs/CAMPAIGN-HARNESS-2026-11-16.md, Part 3) ran the kappa 3, food-on, no-sensing
design for 300,000 steps on 5 seeds and found what 30,000 steps never showed: OSCILLIS treatment winner
shares 0.955, 0.933, 0.843, 0.888, 0.698 (two above the 0.90 takeover bar), VAGRANT one run at 1.000,
MITOSA 0.54 to 0.62; all 15 treatment winners -u; none of 30 neutral runs reached 0.90; neutral shares
settled while treatment shares kept moving. Five seeds, and VAGRANT's envelope was inflated to 0.354 by
two neutral_plus runs drifting to 0.85 and 0.76. Separately, the 30,000-step 2x2 found selection with
food sensing (gain 10) plus repulsion in OSCILLIS (+u) and VAGRANT (-u). This design measures the
long-horizon effect at 50 seeds, with sensing off and on, so takeover becomes a frequency and the sign
question (sensing +u at 30k vs no-sensing -u at 300k in OSCILLIS) is answered on the same runs.

## Design

Cells: food_sense {0, 10} x kappa 3. Species: OSCILLIS, MITOSA, VAGRANT. Seeds 1-50, arms treat /
neutral_plus / neutral_minus, food on (same parameters as the earlier docs), per_channel transport,
300,000 steps, sampled every 1,000, frames every 50,000. 900 runs. Batches: seeds split 1-17 / 18-34 /
35-50 per (species, cell) so each container holds 48 to 51 runs and finishes inside 6 h (manifests
gpu/horizon300k_s1.json, _s2.json, _s3.json; same experiment name, so the three runs write one tree and
the summary reads all seeds together).

## Rules, fixed before the runs (part A's rules scaled to 50 seeds)

1. Takeover frequency per species and cell: treatment runs with winner share at the end >= 0.90, out
   of 50, with an exact binomial 95% interval; neutral runs reaching 0.90 at any sample, out of 100
   (expected 0). "Takeover occurs" if the treatment count exceeds the neutral count with a two-sided
   exact test p < 0.05.
2. Selection at 300,000 steps by the standard rule: E = largest neutral |delta| among the cell's 100
   neutral runs (primary) and the 95th percentile (secondary, labelled); selection if at least 40 of 50
   treatment seeds are outside E with the same winner; takeover if selection and the winner's median
   final share >= 0.90; large but inconsistent if 40+ outside with disagreeing winners.
3. Direction: the sign split of winners among treatment seeds outside E, per cell; the sensing-off and
   sensing-on cells compared per species (same sign, opposite sign, or no consistent sign).
4. Share movement: per treatment run, |delta| at 30,000 vs 300,000 classified grows / saturated /
   reverses (part A's classes); counts per cell against the neutral runs' counts.
5. Distinctness at 300,000 steps: end hybrid fraction per run; "holds" if the median over treatment
   runs stays below half the matched 30,000-step median of the same runs' own series.
6. Neutral-arm sanity: mean neutral delta within 2 SE of zero per cell; a cell outside is flagged as
   harness asymmetry and reported, not voiding.
7. Conservation: max relative drift of sum(body)+sum(food) per run (expect ~1e-14 at 300k steps).

## Predictions

(i) Takeover in at least a fifth of OSCILLIS and VAGRANT treatment runs with sensing off and about
none in neutral runs: a heritable genome difference plus repulsion plus time produces takeover with no
fitness function and no sensing. (ii) The treatment takeover count does not exceed the neutral count:
part A's 3 of 15 was luck. (iii) Sensing changes the winning direction (OSCILLIS +u with sensing, -u
without) or the frequency; or it does not, and time dominates. What consumes the result: the owner's
Dish project, where "movement is a heritable problem-selection policy" is the physics claim; a null
here leaves the kernel as the only fitness, a positive makes the renderer carry part of the selection.
