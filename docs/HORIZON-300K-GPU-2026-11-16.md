# 300,000-step horizon at 50 seeds, with and without food sensing, on GPU (design, 2026-11-16)

Written before any run. Staged for Modal A100 containers (gpu/modal_campaign.py, timeout raised to 6 h
for this experiment); launch needs the owner's yes and a restored Modal workspace.

## Why

Campaign part A (docs/CAMPAIGN-HARNESS-2026-11-16.md, Part 3) ran the kappa 3, food-on, no-sensing
design for 300,000 steps on 5 seeds and found what 30,000 steps never showed: OSCILLIS treatment winner
shares 0.955, 0.933, 0.843, 0.888, 0.698 (two above the 0.90 takeover bar), VAGRANT one run at 1.000,
MITOSA 0.54 to 0.62; all 15 treatment winners -u; none of 30 neutral runs reached 0.90; neutral shares
settled while treatment shares kept moving. Five seeds, and VAGRANT's envelope was inflated to 0.354 by
two neutral_plus runs drifting to 0.85 and 0.76. Separately, the 30,000-step 2x2 found selection with
food sensing (gain 10) plus repulsion in OSCILLIS (+u) and VAGRANT (-u). This design measures the
long-horizon effect at 50 seeds, with sensing off and on, so takeover becomes a frequency and the sign
question (sensing +u at 30k vs no-sensing -u at 300k in OSCILLIS) is answered on the same runs.

## Design

Cells: food_sense {0, 10} x kappa 3. Species: OSCILLIS, MITOSA, VAGRANT. Seeds 1-50, arms treat /
neutral_plus / neutral_minus, food on (same parameters as the earlier docs), per_channel transport,
300,000 steps, sampled every 1,000, frames every 50,000. 900 runs. Batches: seeds split 1-17 / 18-34 /
35-50 per (species, cell) so each container holds 48 to 51 runs and finishes inside 6 h (manifests
gpu/horizon300k_s1.json, _s2.json, _s3.json; same experiment name, so the three runs write one tree and
the summary reads all seeds together).

## Rules, fixed before the runs (part A's rules scaled to 50 seeds)

1. Takeover frequency per species and cell: treatment runs with winner share at the end >= 0.90, out
   of 50, with an exact binomial 95% interval; neutral runs reaching 0.90 at any sample, out of 100
   (expected 0). "Takeover occurs" if the treatment count exceeds the neutral count with a two-sided
   exact test p < 0.05.
2. Selection at 300,000 steps by the standard rule: E = largest neutral |delta| among the cell's 100
   neutral runs (primary) and the 95th percentile (secondary, labelled); selection if at least 40 of 50
   treatment seeds are outside E with the same winner; takeover if selection and the winner's median
   final share >= 0.90; large but inconsistent if 40+ outside with disagreeing winners.
3. Direction: the sign split of winners among treatment seeds outside E, per cell; the sensing-off and
   sensing-on cells compared per species (same sign, opposite sign, or no consistent sign).
4. Share movement: per treatment run, |delta| at 30,000 vs 300,000 classified grows / saturated /
   reverses (part A's classes); counts per cell against the neutral runs' counts.
5. Distinctness at 300,000 steps: end hybrid fraction per run; "holds" if the median over treatment
   runs stays below half the matched 30,000-step median of the same runs' own series.
6. Neutral-arm sanity: mean neutral delta within 2 SE of zero per cell; a cell outside is flagged as
   harness asymmetry and reported, not voiding.
7. Conservation: max relative drift of sum(body)+sum(food) per run (expect ~1e-14 at 300k steps).

## Predictions

(i) Takeover in at least a fifth of OSCILLIS and VAGRANT treatment runs with sensing off and about
none in neutral runs: a heritable genome difference plus repulsion plus time produces takeover with no
fitness function and no sensing. (ii) The treatment takeover count does not exceed the neutral count:
part A's 3 of 15 was luck. (iii) Sensing changes the winning direction (OSCILLIS +u with sensing, -u
without) or the frequency; or it does not, and time dominates. What consumes the result: the owner's
Dish project, where "movement is a heritable problem-selection policy" is the physics claim; a null
here leaves the kernel as the only fitness, a positive makes the renderer carry part of the selection.

## Cost, stated before launch

18 containers (3 species x 2 cells x 3 seed ranges), 48-51 runs each, 300,000 steps: at the measured
19 to 40 ms per batched step (batch ~50, sensing-on cells slower) each container runs 5,700 to 12,000 s,
about $5 to $10 at $3.13 per container-hour (A100 + 8 cores + 32 GiB): **$90 to $190 total**, estimate.
Runs resume on re-launch (finished JSON skipped).

Launch (owner's yes first, Modal restored): for s in 1 2 3: `modal run gpu/modal_campaign.py --manifest
gpu/horizon300k_s$s.json`. Pull: `modal volume get flux-lenia-gpu-out /horizon300k_20261116 ./out/`
(or the JSON-only puller). Summary: scripts/campaign_harness_summary.py's part-3 logic scaled to 50

### Offload plan (prepared, not launched)

Added 2026-11-17 (box 1 at 92/900 done, about 8 runs/h; box 2 scan at about 3,600/7,200). Nothing below has been run live; only `--dry-run` was exercised.

- **Trigger:** a cron runs `/home/thomas/worktrees/flux-lenia-cpu-20261117/scripts/offload_horizon_tail.sh` (no flag). It launches only if box 2's `runs_v2.log` has `ALL_DONE` and no `food_selection.py` runs there, box 1's `runs_h1.log` has fewer than 900 ` done ` lines and no `ALL_DONE`, `engine/*.py scripts/*.py` hash equal on both boxes, and neither marker exists (`/root/fl/out/OFFLOAD_LAUNCHED` on box 2, `.OFFLOAD_LAUNCHED` in the worktree, untracked). Otherwise it prints why and exits 0. Log: `/home/thomas/work/flux-cpu-20261117/offload.log`.
- **Job list:** `campaign_cpu/horizon_jobs.txt` (box 1 uses its copy `/root/fl/horizon_jobs.txt`; `/root/fl/code` has no `campaign_cpu/`) minus jobs with a `done ... rc=0` line in box 1's `runs_h1.log` minus jobs with a `launch` line and no `done` line (in flight). Jobs match on the exact fields `run_batch.py` logs (`SPECIES SEED ARM FOOD KAPPA SUBDIR FOOD_SENSE`). A job that finished with rc != 0 stays in the list.
- **Reverse-order rule:** box 2 takes the END of the remaining list and runs it last-to-first, so the two schedulers approach each other from opposite ends.
- **Skip logic found in `run_batch.py`:** none. It reads the job file once at start, never checks whether an output JSON exists, and `food_selection.py` overwrites `<species>_s<seed>_<arm>_food1.json`. So box 1 will not skip jobs that box 2 finished, and pushing box 2's JSON back to box 1 would not help; the collector does no reverse push.
- **Duplicate-bounding choice:** box 2 gets at most `ceil(remaining x 16/48)` jobs from the tail (concurrency 16 vs 32, so about one third of the remaining work). Box 1 works its list head to tail and would only reach those jobs after finishing the first two thirds, about when box 2 finishes its third; at that point box 1's tail is duplicate work. Trade-off: a smaller share than the whole reversed list means box 2 idles for a while near the end if box 1 is slower than modelled, but no job runs twice unless box 1 is left running past the meeting point. Closing that gap (stopping box 1's scheduler once the collector shows 900 JSON, in-flight runs allowed to finish) is a manual step; the offload script never touches box 1.
- **Output isolation (deviation from "same output dir"):** box 2 runs with output dir `/root/fl/out_h2`, log `/root/fl/out_h2/runs_h2.log`, stdout `/root/fl/out_h2.stdout`. The scan's `/root/fl/out` on box 2 already has the same `<species>_fs<fs>_k3_per_channel` subdirs and the same `*_food1.json` names (30k steps), so launching there would overwrite scan results and append to its per-run logs. Subdir names inside `out_h2` equal box 1's, so the collector maps them into `outputs/horizon300k_20261117/` unchanged.
- **Launch (as box 1):** `source /root/fl/env.sh; cd /root/fl/code && STEPS=300000 SAMPLE_EVERY=1000 FRAME_EVERY=50000 SHARD=_h2 setsid nohup $PY scripts/run_batch.py /root/fl/out_h2 per_channel 16 /root/fl/horizon_tail_h2.txt > /root/fl/out_h2.stdout 2>&1 < /dev/null & disown`.
- **Collector:** `scripts/collect_cpu_campaign.sh` also rsyncs box 2's `/root/fl/out_h2/` (when it exists) into the horizon dir, `--ignore-existing`, JSON/NPZ only after 2 min plus all logs including `runs_h2.log`. Same filename = same job, so the JSON count is unique files and the first copy wins. If box 1 is stopped at the meeting point, the horizon counts as done at 900 JSON with a `runs_h2.log` present.
